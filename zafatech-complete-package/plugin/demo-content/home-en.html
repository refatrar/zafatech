Board-level lab

When the fix goes deeper than a part swap.

Some faults live on the logic board itself — a damaged connector, a failed power chip, corrosion after a spill. That's where micro-soldering comes in: finding the faulty component and repairing it, instead of replacing the whole device.

  • Motherboard repair

    Fault-finding on no-power, boot-loop and no-image boards.

  • Micro-soldering

    Precise component work under magnification.

  • Connector repair

    Charging ports, FPC connectors, HDMI and USB sockets.

  • Charging IC faults

    Devices that won't charge even with a new port or battery.

  • Liquid damage

    Cleaning, corrosion treatment and component repair.

  • Hardware data recovery

    Reviving a board long enough to rescue your data.

Not every board can be saved. We'll tell you honestly after diagnosis — before you commit to anything.
